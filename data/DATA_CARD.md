# Synthetic market data

`synthetic_market.csv` is generated by `core.make_data`, seed 19: 360 business-day timestamps, artificial positive prices, and a bounded artificial sentiment feature. The simulator injects a weak lagged sentiment effect into returns. There are no real companies, market prices, news articles or external data licenses. Business days do not model exchange holidays.

The feature at date t is assumed available by close t. For real news, retain article source, license, publication and ingestion timestamps; aggregate only information available by that origin, handle timezone/corporate-action adjustments, and use a genuinely untouched chronological test period. Never use future news or revised data. This demo does not implement live news ingestion or text sentiment estimation. MAE is in synthetic price units. Transaction costs, trading strategy, predictive intervals and profits are not evaluated.
